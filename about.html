About Us

A family kitchen that grew into a restaurant — and never stopped cooking like one.

Since February 2, 2024

The House of Zheng

A passion for cooking started all of this. Chef Zheng began by making creative dishes for his family, and the time and effort he put into them was how he showed love to the people around him.

That passion kept growing, and eventually Hao Hao Chi opened in Tupelo — the same food, the same care, just a much bigger table.
